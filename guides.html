👹 Boss Fights

Bosses are how you get most Warframes in this game. Nearly every planet's assassination mission has a boss, and that boss drops the components (neuroptics, chassis, systems) for one specific Warframe. Kill them, build the parts in your Foundry, buy the main blueprint from the Market for credits, and you've got a new frame.

How bossing actually works

  • One boss, one frame. Each assassination target is tied to a Warframe. Check any frame's page here to see who drops it.
  • It's a grind by design. Each part has its own drop chance, so expect several runs. This is normal — put on a podcast.
  • Bring the right damage. Most bosses have Alloy Armor or robotic health, which means Radiation damage (Heat + Electricity) shreds them. Corrosive works too.
  • Damage attenuation is real. Bosses take reduced damage from huge single hits — this stops one-shot cheese. Sustained DPS beats big burst numbers here.
  • Respect invulnerability phases. Many bosses go invulnerable and make you do a mechanic first (shoot the glowing bit, wait out the phase). Don't waste ammo — learn the pattern once and it becomes routine.

Bosses worth knowing

  • Jackal (Venus, Fossa) — drops Rhino. Your first real tank frame. Every new player should farm this.
  • Captain Vor (Mercury, Tolstoj) — tutorial boss, drops Seer parts. Easy.
  • Lech Kril (Mars, War) — drops Excalibur. Has a backpack weak point mechanic.
  • Alad V (Jupiter, Themisto) — drops Valkyr. Bring Radiation.
  • Raptors (Europa, Naamah) — drops Nova. Fast fight, fun farm.
  • Sargas Ruk (Saturn, Tethys) — drops Ember. Shoot the glowing weak points when they open.
  • Tyl Regor (Uranus, Titania) — drops Equinox. Longest farm in the game (8 parts across day/night aspects) — pace yourself.
  • Kela De Thaym (Sedna, Merrow) — drops Saryn. Requires Judgment Points from Rathuum arena to even enter.
  • Vay Hek (Earth, Everest) — drops Hydroid. Annoying drone phase; bring patience.
  • Lephantis (Deimos, Magnacidium) — drops Nekros. Multi-head fight, each head has a weak point.
  • Ambulas (Pluto, Hades) — drops Trinity. Needs Animo Beacons from Ambulas events on Corpus ship missions.
  • Mutalist Alad V (Eris — key required) — drops Mesa. You need coordinates from Infested Invasions to build the key.
  • Hyena Pack (Neptune, Psamathe) — drops Loki. Four hyenas, bring crowd control.

Bossing tips

  • Bring a tanky frame (Rhino, Inaros, Revenant) or something with survivability until you learn the fight.
  • Radiation damage is the default answer for most bosses. Mod for it.
  • If a boss feels impossible, you're probably missing the mechanic — check the wiki or ask the Vault AI.
  • Some frames don't come from bosses at all — check quests, bounties, and dojo research. The Warframes page lists every farm location.

🔪 Kuva Liches

A Kuva Lich is your own personal Grineer nemesis. You create it, it taunts you, it steals your mission rewards, it levels up every time you fail to kill it — and eventually you hunt it down for a powerful Kuva weapon. It's one of the best systems in the game once you understand the loop.

Step 1 — Spawn your Lich

  • Go to any level 20+ Grineer mission (Saturn and up works).
  • Kill enemies until a Kuva Larvling is marked — your screen will flash and Lotus will announce it.
  • Find the Larvling and get it to low health. It kneels. A weapon hovers above its head — this is the weapon your Lich will carry. Don't like it? Leave the mission and try again.
  • Perform a Mercy kill (parazon) on it. Your Lich is born, with a name, personality, and a grudge.

Step 2 — Learn the Requiem sequence

  • Your Lich has a hidden sequence of 3 Requiem mods in a specific order.
  • Kill the Lich's Thralls (marked enemies in Lich-controlled missions) to gather Requiem Murmurs — these reveal which mods are in the sequence (but not the order).
  • Equip Requiem mods on your Parazon (in the mod screen, Requiem tab). You get Requiem mods from Requiem Relics, cracked in Kuva Fortress fissures.
  • Trial and error: confront your Lich, use the Parazon, and guess the order. Wrong order = the Lich ranks up, gets stronger, and leaves. Right order = first mod sticks, keep going.
  • Pro tip: the game tells you which of your three guesses was wrong, so it's a logic puzzle, not pure guessing.

Step 3 — Vanquish or Convert

  • Once all three Requiems land in the right order, your Lich is downed. Choose:
    • Vanquish — kill it, take its Kuva weapon.
    • Convert — spare it, and it becomes an ally that sometimes shows up in your missions to help. You still get the weapon either way? No — converting means you DON'T get the weapon this time, but a converted Lich can be traded to other players.
  • Kuva weapons come with a bonus element (Heat, Toxin, Cold, etc.) determined by the Warframe that landed the killing blow on the Larvling — the "progenitor." Pick your progenitor frame to pick your element. Heat and Toxin are the popular picks.
  • Ephemeras: Liches sometimes have a cosmetic Ephemera. You'll see it on the Larvling screen — if you want it, that's the Lich to hunt.
  • Valence Fusion: got a duplicate weapon with a low bonus %? Fuse it into your good one in your orbiter to raise the bonus toward 60%.

Is it worth it?

Yes. Kuva weapons (Bramma, Zarr, Nukor, Chakkhurr) are some of the strongest in the game. The system looks complicated but after your first Lich it becomes routine: spawn, murmur farm, guess the order, collect weapon. Ask the Vault AI for build advice once you have yours — the Builds page has Kuva weapon setups.

⚙️ Sisters of Parvos

Sisters are the Corpus version of Liches — same nemesis system, different flavor. Instead of Kuva weapons you get Tenet weapons, and instead of a Grineer bruiser you get a Corpus executive with a robotic Hound companion.

Step 1 — Spawn your Sister

  • Get a Zenith Granum Crown from a Treasurer (Corpus missions — kill the special Corpus unit that spawns).
  • Go to a Corpus ship tileset mission and find the glowing golden hand terminal. Use the Crown to enter the Granum Void.
  • Inside, kill 75 enemies before time runs out. This is the skill check — bring a strong loadout. Each rank of Crown changes the difficulty and the Sister's level.
  • A Candidate appears. Down her and Mercy-kill her to create your Sister. Like Liches, she shows her weapon first — check it before you commit.

Step 2 — The Requiem loop

Identical to Kuva Liches: gather murmurs from Hounds and Thralls, figure out the 3-mod Requiem sequence, confront her. Wrong order = she ranks up. Right order = she's downed.

Step 3 — Vanquish or Convert

  • Vanquish — take her Tenet weapon (Arca Plasmor, Diplos, Exec, Tetra are standouts).
  • Convert — she becomes an ally, and you can also claim her Hound — a modular robotic companion you can customize.
  • Tenet weapons also have progenitor-based bonus elements, same as Kuva weapons. Valence Fusion works the same way.

Lich or Sister first?

Dealer's choice. Kuva weapons skew toward raw damage (Bramma, Zarr), Tenet weapons have great utility picks (Arca Plasmor, Diplos). Both systems use the same Requiem mods, so progress in one teaches you the other. If you can only do one, most players start with Liches because the missions are simpler.

🎲 Riven Mods

Rivens are special mods with randomized stats for one specific weapon. A great Riven can take a good weapon to absurd heights; a bad one is a paperweight. They're the endgame min-max chase — exciting, but completely optional.

What is a Riven?

  • Each Riven is locked to one specific weapon (e.g. "Kuva Zarr Riven").
  • It rolls 2–3 positive stats and sometimes 1 negative stat. A negative actually boosts the positives, so a harmless negative (like -zoom or -recoil) is ideal.
  • Rivens start veiled — you must complete the challenge written on them to unveil (e.g. "kill 30 enemies with headshots without missing"). The challenge hints at the weapon type.
  • You can re-roll stats with Kuva. Each re-roll costs more Kuva than the last (it caps). This is the Kuva sink of the game.

How to get them

  • Sorties — daily missions, chance of a Riven as a reward. This is the classic source.
  • Steel Path dailies — Steel Essence shop and daily missions.
  • Archon Hunts — weekly, can drop Rivens.
  • Nightwave — sometimes in the reward track.
  • Trading with other players (they're tradable once unveiled — veiled ones too).

What makes a good Riven?

  • God-tier positives: critical chance, critical damage, multishot, raw damage. These multiply everything.
  • Good: elemental damage, fire rate, reload speed (quality of life that adds up).
  • Harmless negatives: -zoom, -recoil, -ammo maximum, faction-specific damage negatives. These pump your positives for free.
  • Bad negatives: -damage, -multishot, -crit chance — these can make a Riven worse than a normal mod.
  • Riven disposition (the 1–5 dots on the weapon's stats page) controls how strong the numbers are. Popular meta weapons have LOW disposition (weak Rivens); off-meta favorites have HIGH disposition (monster Rivens). This is why Rivens reward using weapons you love rather than whatever's meta.

Beginner advice

  • Don't stress about Rivens early. They're an endgame optimization, not a requirement. Normal mods will carry you through the entire star chart and into Steel Path.
  • When you get your first veiled Riven, do the challenge — it's usually easier than it sounds.
  • Never re-roll a Riven on a weapon you don't actually use. Kuva is precious.
  • A "bad" Riven for a weapon you love still beats no Riven. Disposition favors the bold.

🧠 Game Knowledge Hub

Everything worth knowing, in one place. The systems that confuse new players, explained straight.

Damage types — what beats what

  • Impact / Puncture / Slash (IPS) — physical damage. Slash is king because Slash procs bypass armor with true damage over time.
  • Heat, Cold, Electricity, Toxin — base elements. Combine them for advanced elements.
  • Viral (Cold + Toxin) — increases damage enemies take. The meta pick for almost everything. Pair with Heat for armor stripping.
  • Corrosive (Electricity + Toxin) — strips armor directly. Great against Grineer.
  • Radiation (Heat + Electricity) — bonus vs Alloy Armor (bosses) and confuses enemies.
  • Magnetic (Cold + Electricity) — anti-shield, niche. Mostly for Corpus-heavy missions.
  • Gas, Blast — situational, mostly for fun builds.
  • Rule of thumb: Viral + Heat for general use, Corrosive for heavy Grineer, Radiation for bosses.

Modding basics

  • Base damage first: Serration (rifles), Hornet Strike (pistols), Pressure Point (melee), plus elemental mods. These multiply everything else.
  • Multishot (Split Chamber, Barrel Diffusion, Lethal Torrent) — more projectiles = more damage. Always worth it.
  • Crit: only invest if the weapon has 18%+ base crit chance. Point Strike + Vital Sense is the classic combo.
  • Status: only invest if 20%+ base status chance. 60/60 mods (like Rime Rounds) give status + element efficiently.
  • Warframes: Vitality/Redirection for survivability, Intensify/Continuity/Stretch/Flow as the core stat mods, then specialize. Steel Fiber if the frame has high armor.
  • Don't spread thin. Max a few key mods before dabbling. A rank 10 Serration beats five half-ranked mods.
  • See the Mods page for what every mod does and where it drops.

Mastery Rank

  • Leveling new Warframes, weapons, and companions to 30 earns Mastery XP. Higher MR = more loadout slots, more daily trades, more standing cap.
  • MR tests unlock every couple ranks — practice them first (there's a practice option!). Failing just means waiting 24 hours.
  • Level everything, even "bad" gear. It's all Mastery fodder.

Steel Path

  • Unlocks after completing every node on the star chart. Enemies get +100 levels, more armor, more health — but drop Steel Essence.
  • Don't rush it. If normal star chart missions feel easy and your builds are solid, you're ready to try.
  • Steel Essence buys Arcane Adapters, Umbra Forma blueprints, and cosmetics from Teshin.
  • The Builds page has dedicated Steel Path and one-shot setups.

Arbitrations

  • Endless missions with stacking difficulty, unlocked after all nodes. Enemies get buffed; you get one life (teammates can revive you with tokens).
  • Rewards Vitus Essence — spend it on Archon Shards, Aura mods, and cosmetics.
  • Bring a self-sufficient frame. Dying wastes everyone's time.

Trading

  • Unlocks at MR2. Trade prime parts, mods, Rivens, and more with other players for platinum.
  • Use warframe.market to check prices — never guess. The Trading page has templates and a full how-to.
  • Maroo's Bazaar or clan dojos are the trading spots. Both players must be present.
  • Never trade in a rush. Scammers prey on hurry.

Clans & Dojos

  • Join a clan. Dojos have exclusive weapon/Warframe research you can't get anywhere else.
  • Contribute resources when you can — it unlocks gear for everyone.
  • Trading happens in dojos too, so it's doubly useful.

Glossary — talk like a Tenno

  • Forma — resets an item to 30 to change mod polarities. The path to real builds.
  • Potato — Orokin Reactor (frames) / Orokin Catalyst (weapons). Doubles mod capacity. Always worth it on gear you love.
  • Exilus — extra mod slot for utility mods (unlocked with an Exilus Adapter).
  • Helminth — the ship system that lets you swap one ability between Warframes (by sacrificing a frame).
  • Primed mods — Baro Ki'Teer's supercharged versions. Expensive but best-in-slot.
  • Galvanized mods — Arbitration vendor upgrades that stack on kill. Steel Path staples.
  • SP — Steel Path. ESO/SO — Elite/Sanctuary Onslaught. Tridolon — triple Eidolon hunt.
  • WTS/WTB/WTT — Want To Sell / Buy / Trade (trade chat shorthand).
  • Radshare — everyone brings a Radiant relic to a fissure for best rewards.
  • Plat — platinum, the premium currency (freely tradable between players).
