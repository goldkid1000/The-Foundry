🌱 New Player Guide

Your first Warframe

You'll pick one of three starters: Excalibur (balanced sword fighter — best all-rounder), Mag (shield control, great vs Corpus), or Volt (speed + electric damage). Honestly? Excalibur is the safest pick — simple, strong, and his Exalted Blade carries you far.

What to do first

1. Clear the star chart planet by planet. Each blue node you finish unlocks the next. Don't rush — you'll naturally collect resources and mods.
2. Do the junctions. The requirements (like "kill 50 enemies with...") teach you mechanics and unlock new planets.
3. Finish the main quests in order: Vor's Prize → Once Awake → The Archwing → Stolen Dreams → The New Strange → Natah → The Second Dream → The War Within. The story gets really good — don't skip cutscenes.

How mods work (the 30-second version)

Mods are cards you slot into your gear. Each has a drain cost (top-right number) and your gear has limited capacity. Level up your mods at the mod station with Endo — a rank 5 Vitality beats five unranked mods. Polarity slots (the little symbols) halve the cost of matching mods. Priority: base damage → multishot → crit/status → elements for weapons; health → armor → ability stats for warframes.

When to start trading

Once you can crack Void Relics (around Mars/Phobos), start saving prime parts. Check prices on the price checker, list on warframe.market, and trade in-game at Maroo's Bazaar or your clan dojo. Even 10-20 platinum buys you weapon/warframe slots — the most important purchase in the game.

Beginner builds to try

Rhino (tank): Vitality + Steel Fiber + Intensify — press 2 (Iron Skin) and you're nearly unkillable.
Hek (shotgun): Point Blank + Hell's Chamber + two elemental 60/60s — deletes everything through Saturn.
Excalibur: Vitality + Intensify + Continuity — Exalted Blade (press 4) with a melee build.
